Publication
April 2026

GLORIA Trial Expansion Arm — Nature Communications

The expansion arm of the GLORIA trial evaluated radiotherapy in combination with NOX-A12, a CXCL12-neutralizing aptamer, and bevacizumab in patients with newly diagnosed, MGMT-unmethylated glioblastoma. All patients in this cohort achieved a radiographic response, including one complete response. Median overall survival was 19.9 months, compared with 12.7 months for radiotherapy and NOX-A12 alone.

Medwave supports the study's advanced imaging component, including imaging workflow support and central imaging review activities.

May 2024

GLORIA Trial Dose Escalation — Nature Communications

The dose-escalation phase of the ongoing GLORIA trial, published in Nature Communications, evaluated NOX-A12 — a CXCL12-neutralizing aptamer — combined with radiotherapy in ten patients with newly-diagnosed, MGMT-unmethylated glioblastoma. The combination was well-tolerated with no dose-limiting toxicities, and 90% of patients showed a radiographic response.

Medwave supports the study's advanced imaging component, including imaging workflow support and central imaging review activities.
